## Prop up your phone.

Yoginini watches your form through the camera and speaks gentle corrections out
loud, the way a good teacher would across the room.

A pose model running **on the device** tracks 33 body landmarks, turns them into
the angles between your limbs, compares those to a reference a teacher recorded,
and when something drifts a calm voice says so. One cue at a time. Never off a
single frame. Never twice in five seconds.

When you want a human, you book one by the hour.

> **Yoga has been taught one way for a few thousand years:** a teacher watches,
> and says a little. We built a machine that can do the watching so more people
> can be taught, then set rules so it behaves like a good teacher would.

## No video ever leaves your phone

This is the whole design, not a paragraph in a policy.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Camera frames** | Analysed on the device, discarded frame by frame |
| **Images uploaded** | **Zero.** None are stored, so none can leak |
| **What does leave** | Joint angles, pose scores, session metadata |
| **Why angles, not positions** | An angle describes a shape. It cannot be turned back into a picture of a room |
| **Sharing with a coach** | Off by default, chosen per scope, revocable at any time |
| **Leaderboards** | None exist anywhere in the product |

A practice done in a bedroom deserves that.

## Real teachers, by the hour

The AI fills the days between classes. A human does what no model can: notice
you. Coaches record their own poses, set per-joint tolerances, and write the
cues in their own words — and every pose is reviewed by a second teacher before
a student ever sees it.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Coaches set their own rate | typically **$50–85 / hr** |
| Coach keeps | **80%** |
| Repeat student, same coach | our share drops to **10–12%** |
| Membership | **$3** for 14 days, then **$15** / month |

**Teaching yoga?** The founding cohort is open —
[apply here](https://yoginini.us/coaches/#apply). Founding coaches help shape
the core library and pay a reduced fee for their first year.

## Status: nothing has shipped

We would rather say this plainly than let a landing page imply otherwise.

- **No app exists.** No App Store or Play Store listing, and no link pretending
  there is one. iPhone first, Android after.
- **No coaches are signed.** The site publishes zero coach profiles — what it
  shows are the open seats in the founding cohort.
- **No accounts, no checkout, no payment field** anywhere on the site.
- **No usage or member figures** appear anywhere, and none will until something
  real counts them.

The waitlist is the only thing that is real today.
[Join it](https://yoginini.us) and we will write once, when your mat is ready.

## The name, and the colour

A **yogini** is a woman who practises. The name honours the teachers most of us
first learned from, and the small repeated syllable is the practice itself: the
same thing, again, a little better. The `.us` is not a place — it is who is on
the mat with you.

The mark is **Sahasrara**, the crown chakra: the seventh and highest, drawn as a
thousand-petalled lotus and coloured violet. It stands for awareness and the
quiet that arrives when the rest of the practice has done its work. That is why
everything here is purple, even on the days it is mostly about a stiff left hip.
